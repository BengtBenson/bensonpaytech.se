Services

Hands-on development combined with architecture, security and project leadership – in the areas where payments, hardware and regulation meet.

Payment terminal integration

Integrating card terminals with POS systems on Android – both on-device and off-device (ECR) setups. Sale, refund, reversal, part payment, receipts, reconciliation and the recovery cases that only show up on real hardware.

Verifone · PAX · Nets/Baxi · Nexo · Android

Fiscal control units

Connecting cash registers to certified fiscal control units according to the Swedish cash register regulations (kassaregisterlagen). I know the requirements, the protocols and the pitfalls of getting a POS solution approved and running.

Control units · Receipts · Compliance

About

I'm Bengt Benson. I have worked with payments for more than 30 years – much of it in parking and public transport, with payment and ticketing systems for web, apps and unattended machines. In 2020 I founded Benson Pay Tech AB.

Today I combine consulting with a part-time role as software system architect and PCI expert in card issuing at a Swedish bank. My consulting work is centered on payment terminal and fiscal integrations for point-of-sale systems – from the first line of code to certified releases running in stores.

Alongside the technical work, I have a background in management, board work and politics, which helps me see the whole picture: business, security, regulation and the people involved.
